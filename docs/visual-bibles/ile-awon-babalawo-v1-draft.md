## Deity link

**Ilé Àwọn Babaláwo has NO deity linked in the catalogue.**

This rule sheet neither creates nor assumes such a link. Rule 41 explicitly
forbids materialising Ọ̀rúnmìlà, Èṣù, Olódùmárè or any named Òrìṣà as a
humanlike or supernatural character in this House *under the current catalogue
state*. If leadership later decides a deity association belongs here, that is a
separate catalogue decision and this Visual Bible must be re-reviewed.

## Production purpose

This Visual Bible governs the **personalised recorded online prayer**
environment for Ilé Àwọn Babaláwo. It controls what the Sacred House looks like
and how visual performance behaves. It does **not** control what the Babaláwo
says.

The locked production identity is:

- There is normally **one** physically present human: the approved
  Babaláwo/practitioner.
- The person receiving the prayer is **online**. The camera/lens represents the
  remote recipient.
- There must **not** be a second physical client sitting opposite the
  Babaláwo.
- Direct lens contact is occasional and reserved for personal-address moments,
  never a continuous stare.
- The approved environment is the **same physical Sacred House across all
  shots**; the room is not redesigned from scene to scene.

### What is governed elsewhere — deliberately absent from this document

- Prayer wording is governed by approved spiritual content and Prayer Session
  Templates. No prayer text appears here.
- Approved audio/TTS, storyboard construction and Remotion assembly are
  separate layers.
- No ritual instructions, preparation instructions or call-and-response text
  are defined here.

## Visual reference authority

Six approved reference images form the Ilé Àwọn Babaláwo continuity pack.
`babalawo 1.png` is the **primary master room reference**; images 2–6 are
supporting continuity references showing the same production environment from
different camera positions.

The images establish visual production identity. They are **not** documentary
proof of every depicted object, symbol or ritual. Incidental AI-generated
background figures, carvings, containers, wall objects and decorative motifs
visible in them are **not** independently canonised, and no spiritual meaning
may be inferred from them.

Authority order, highest first:

1. Repository schema and technical constraints
2. The locked 78-rule written source below
3. `babalawo 1.png` master reference
4. Supporting images 2–6
5. Incidental generated visual details — **not authoritative**

Where the images and the written rules appear to conflict, **the written rules
take precedence**. In particular: unnamed background figures are not to be
identified as specific Òrìṣà, no deity link may be derived from an image, no
ritual action may be derived from a body pose, and the direct-to-camera
open-hand gesture seen in one reference is an example, **not** a mandatory
prayer gesture.
